What it does

Everything between a blank form and a filled one.

One workflow. Each part works on its own, and together they take a form you have never trained on to a model you can measure.

Reads any form

Paste the HTML or a list of fields. AIrForms works out what each field means, from postal codes to policy numbers, and flags the ones it is unsure of.

Sample data without real data

Companies cannot share customer records. AIrForms invents coherent, realistic ones from the form alone, with identifiers that cannot belong to a real person.

Learns to autofill

Train on those records with the engine you choose. The model fills a field only when it is confident, and says why, so agents can trust what they see.

Simulates the saving

Play the model against forms it has never seen. Every keystroke is counted, so the saving is a number you can take to a meeting, not a promise.

Chat and voice

Bot templates turn "I moved to Austin" into a filled address change. Typed, spoken or clicked, every request arrives the same way.

Fits into your apps

A versioned REST API, bearer tokens and a dependency-free JavaScript client, so your own application can ask a trained model what to put in a form.

How it works

From form to filled, in five steps.

  1. 1

    Source

    Bring in the form: its markup, a field list, or a bundled example.

  2. 2

    Schema

    Check what each field means and which fields follow from others.

  3. 3

    Generate

    Make as many coherent sample records as you need, checked before you see them.

  4. 4

    Train

    Teach a model the form, and see what it can fill and what stays yours.

  5. 5

    Simulate

    Fill unseen forms with it and count exactly what it saved.

Security

Built for places where data cannot leave.

AIrForms was designed for locked-down environments. There is nothing to install beyond Python, nothing that phones home, and the only feature that talks to a network is one you switch on yourself.

  • Accounts and roles with hashed passwords and per-person libraries.
  • Synthetic by default: models train on invented records, not customers.
  • Calibrated confidence: a field it cannot predict is left blank.
  • Scoped API tokens you can expire and revoke at any time.
